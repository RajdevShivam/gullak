# Gullak: 3-minute demo script

**Setup:** open https://shivamrajdev.com/gullak/?seed=42 in a **fresh incognito window** (no stale cache). On the title page pick **Mumbai**, **IT** and **No timer, let me think** so you can talk without the clock running. Rehearse once.

Seed 42 with Mumbai + IT (rehearsed on the live site): crash in Year 4, wedding in Year 5, car in Year 8, lottery in Year 11, then a run of family asks in Years 12–16.

**0:00 – Hook (15s)**
"Every Indian kid had a gullak. What if yours had to last 20 real years of Indian markets? We just won't tell you which 20."

**0:15 – Title page (15s)**
Tap Mumbai and IT, type a judge's name. "Their life now: Mumbai pay, Mumbai rent."

**0:30 – Year 1 (25s)**
Point at the ledger: salary savings ₹1.6 L, "Rent in Mumbai −₹36,000", ₹1.2 L left for the gullak. Set about 70% Nifty, 30% FD. Drop it in the gullak.

**0:55 – Fast-forward (15s)**
"Skip 2 years" stops before Year 4 ("Trouble overseas"). "It won't let you sleep through a crash." Point at "Stock B just landed in the family WhatsApp group". Drop it in: about −₹2.7 L, "New worst year".

**1:10 – Panic or hold? (15s)**
Year 5: "Everyone says stay away from shares." Ask the room. Hold. (The Nifty chip on the reveal shows the +76% rebound.)

**1:25 – The wedding and the bill (30s)**
Same year: pick the big fat wedding. The bill pops up: **"₹4.8 L is due. Now."** Read the Nifty row: "Locks in a ₹28,575 loss". Point at the FD row's 10% penalty. "Sell, or put it all on the card at 36% and continue." Mention that with the timer on you'd have 45 seconds. "The emergency-fund lesson, taught by Mummy's guest list."
- Swipe the card here for the funniest ending (see 2:20); sell the FD and Nifty for a respectable one.

**1:55 – To the lottery (20s)**
Skip 2 years → Year 8, the car: take the middle option. Skip 1 → Year 9, skip 2 → **Year 11: the Diwali bumper wins ₹33.7 L**, minus 31.2% tax. Drop it in.

**2:15 – The asks (20s)**
Year 12 sister's wedding (middle option), Year 13 **"Villa, SUV, the works"**, Year 14 **"Back Mama-ji's business"**. "Most of us would. That's the point." Every short year means a bill: card it to keep moving.

**2:35 – The gullak cracks (25s)**
Skip to Year 20 (Papa's surgery and the forty cousins come up on the way: take defaults). Show "You just lived through 2005–2024", the stock reveal (A was Yes Bank…), the podium and its note ("a smaller balance doesn't mean a worse one"), the chart and the 3 takeaways.
- If you carded every bill, Judge finishes **3rd at about −₹93 L**, behind SIP Aunty (₹94.2 L) and FD Uncle (₹62.6 L), with "The credit card always wins" as the top takeaway. Let the room laugh.

Close: "No AI at runtime, no backend. Real history, real lessons, one static page."
