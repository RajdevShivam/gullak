# Gullak: 3-minute demo script

**Setup:** open `http://localhost:5175/?seed=42` (a fixed seed, so the demo is predictable). Rehearse once beforehand and note which chapter has the forced sale.

**0:00 – Hook (15s)**
"Every Indian kid had a gullak. What if yours had to last 20 years of real Indian markets: 2008, demonetisation, COVID, crypto?"

**0:15 – Title page (15s)**
Type a judge's name. "It's their life story now."

**0:30 – Chapter 1 (30s)**
Show the news card, the sliders and the plain-English explainers. Go safe: mostly FD and PPF. Invest, and point out the count-up and the return chips.

**1:00 – ⏩ Fast-forward (20s)**
Fast-forward stops just before 2008. "It won't let you sleep through a crash." Move heavily into Nifty anyway, then take the hit.

**1:20 – Panic or hold? (25s)**
2009: "Experts say stay away." Ask the room whether to hold or sell. Hold, and watch the rebound.

**1:45 – Life happens (20s)**
Show a forced sale or a wedding. "This is the emergency-fund lesson, taught by your cousin's wedding."

**2:05 – ⏩ to the end (20s)**
Fast-forward through COVID and crypto.

**2:25 – The gullak cracks (35s)**
Show the ranking against FD Uncle and SIP Aunty, the chart, and the 3 personalised takeaways. Close with: "No AI at runtime. Real history, real lessons, one static page."
